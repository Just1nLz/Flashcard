How Learn works

Each round has up to 10 core cards plus 3 labeled past-mistake reviews. Mastery means 3 correct answers in a row for the same card, across rounds, or you can mark a card mastered yourself. A wrong answer resets that card to 0/3. Missed questions return later in this round; unmastered cards keep returning in future rounds.

Answers follow your PDF’s key. Practice choices may include added incorrect options when the PDF lists only correct answers. Flashcards keeps the original PDF choices; true/false cards keep their original format. Learning progress saves on this device.
